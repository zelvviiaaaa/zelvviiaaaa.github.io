Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how zelvviiaaaa ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you access or use our applications, services, and Google OAuth integrations.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Google OAuth to authenticate with our application, we may request access to standard Google profile information, including:

  • Basic profile information (such as your name and email address)
  • Unique account identifiers provided by Google

We do not collect or store passwords, financial data, or sensitive personal details beyond what is strictly necessary to authenticate your session.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate and verify your identity
  • To authorize access to our integration workflows and API tools
  • To maintain security and prevent unauthorized access or abuse

3. Google User Data Compliance

Our use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties.
  • We do not use your Google user data for advertising purposes.
  • We do not share your data with third parties except as required by law or strictly necessary to operate the service.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We only retain user information for as long as necessary to provide you with the service. You may revoke access at any time through your Google Account Permissions. Upon account termination or written request, your data will be permanently removed.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard encryption, HTTPS communication, and access controls to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or data handling practices, please contact us at: zelvviiaaaa@gmail.com.
